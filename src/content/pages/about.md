**Daily Word Hunt** is a free browser **word search** (find-the-word / 划词) puzzle.

Open the site → play **today’s** puzzle. No account, no app install, no paywall for the daily game.

## What you get

- **A new puzzle every day** — released by calendar day (site timezone).
- **Simple controls** — drag letter to letter, or tap first then last.
- **Word list + short glosses** — good for a calm break or light vocabulary practice.
- **Print & large print** — classroom-friendly and easier reading.
- **Past days** — revisit earlier puzzles when you want.

## What this is *not*

- Not a fill-in / Wordscapes clone (we don’t ask you to build words from letter racks).
- Not a social network — leaderboards may come later; play works offline-in-browser once loaded.
- Not licensed movie/game IP packs. Themes we publish will use our own or clearly free-to-use word lists.

## How daily puzzles work

Puzzles are prepared ahead of time and stored as static files. When the calendar day changes, the home page loads that day’s puzzle. You don’t need a special URL like `/play/2026-08-06` — just come to the home page.

Optional archive links exist for sharing a specific day; the main product is always **Today**.

## Privacy & tech (short version)

- Playing is client-side: your device loads a puzzle JSON and runs the grid in the browser.
- We aim for a static-first site (fast, simple, cheap to host). Optional scores/leaderboards would be separate and opt-in later.
