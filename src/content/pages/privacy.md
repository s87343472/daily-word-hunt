**Last updated:** August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Daily Word Hunt** (“we,” “the site”) handles information when you visit or use [Daily Word Hunt](/).

This product is designed to be **simple and static-first**. You can play the daily word search **without creating an account**.

## 1. Summary

| Topic | Today’s practice |
|-------|------------------|
| Account | Not required |
| Puzzle play | Runs in your browser after loading public puzzle files |
| Leaderboards / scores | Not live yet; if added later, this policy will be updated |
| Ads | Not part of the core product today |
| Sale of personal data | We do not sell your personal information |

## 2. Information we may process

### 2.1 Information you choose to provide

- **Optional contact** — if you email us or use a future feedback form, we receive whatever you send (for example, your address and message).
- **Optional future features** — if we add nicknames, score submission, or newsletters, we will collect only what those features need and update this page.

### 2.2 Information collected automatically

Like most websites, hosting and delivery networks may process **technical data**, such as:

- IP address  
- Browser type and device class  
- Pages requested, approximate time, and referrer  
- Error or performance logs  

We use this to keep the site running, debug issues, and understand aggregate traffic—not to build a personal profile for sale.

### 2.3 Local data on your device

The game and theme settings may store data **only on your device**, for example:

- Light / dark theme preference  
- Large-print toggle state  
- Optional future local progress (e.g. “completed today”)

This data typically stays in `localStorage` or similar browser storage and is not uploaded unless a future feature explicitly says so.

## 3. Cookies and similar technologies

### 3.1 Your controls

On first visit we show a **cookie banner** so you can:

- **Accept all** — allow optional analytics (when enabled)  
- **Reject optional** — necessary only  
- **Customize** — toggle analytics on or off  

You can reopen choices anytime via **Cookie settings** in the site footer (or `window.__cookieConsent.open()` in the browser console for debugging).

Your choice is stored in **localStorage** under a site-specific key (not used as a third-party tracking cookie). If we change consent categories in a material way, we may ask again (versioned consent).

### 3.2 Categories

| Category | Default | Purpose |
|----------|---------|---------|
| **Necessary** | Always on | Theme preference, storing your consent choice, basic site function |
| **Analytics** | **Off** until you opt in | Aggregate traffic / product improvement — only loaded if you allow it |

### 3.3 What we may use

- **Essential** browser storage for theme or UI preferences  
- **Hosting / CDN** technical logs required to deliver the site  
- **Optional analytics** scripts or beacons — **only after opt-in**. When configured, we may load **Plausible** (`PUBLIC_PLAUSIBLE_DOMAIN`) and/or **Google Analytics 4** (`PUBLIC_GA_MEASUREMENT_ID`). Neither loads until you accept analytics.

We do not use play as a vehicle to track you across unrelated third-party sites. Analytics remain **denied** until you accept.

## 4. How we use information

- Operate and improve Daily Word Hunt  
- Fix bugs and prevent abuse  
- Respond if you contact us  
- Comply with law when required  

## 5. Sharing

We may share limited technical data with:

- **Hosting / CDN providers** (to serve pages and assets)  
- **Professional advisors or authorities** when legally required  

We do **not** sell personal information.

## 6. Retention

- Server/CDN logs: retained according to the host’s defaults or our configuration (often days to months).  
- Contact messages: kept only as long as needed to respond and keep basic records.  
- Local device data: until you clear site data or uninstall the browser profile.

## 7. Children

The daily word lists are general vocabulary and suitable for a broad audience. The site is not directed at children under 13 in a way that knowingly collects personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it where feasible.

## 8. International visitors

The site may be hosted or cached in multiple regions. By using the site, you understand that technical data may be processed where our providers operate.

## 9. Your choices

- Play without an account.  
- Clear cookies and site storage in your browser.  
- Use private / incognito mode.  
- Contact us to ask about data we may hold from emails or future features.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy as the product changes (for example, if we launch scores or analytics). The “Last updated” date will change; material changes will be reflected on this page.

## 11. Contact

For privacy questions, use the email on the [Contact](/contact) page and put **Privacy** in the subject line.
