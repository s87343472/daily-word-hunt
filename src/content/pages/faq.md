Quick answers about the free daily word search. For controls, see [How to play](/how-to). For product background, see [About](/about).

## Playing

### What is Daily Word Hunt?

A free **word search** in your browser. Letters are already on the grid; you find each word from the list by dragging or tapping endpoints.

### Do I need an account?

**No.** Open the home page and play today’s puzzle.

### Where is today’s puzzle?

Always on the **[home page](/)**. You do not need a special dated URL.

### Can I play older puzzles?

Yes — open **[Past puzzles](/past)** and pick a date, or use a shared `/daily/YYYY-MM-DD` link.

### Why did the puzzle change overnight?

A new puzzle is **released by calendar day** (site timezone). When the date changes, the home page loads that day’s grid.

### Drag doesn’t work well on my phone. What now?

Use **tap first letter → tap last letter**. Same straight-line rule, often easier on small screens. Details: [How to play](/how-to).

### Which directions are allowed?

Horizontal, vertical, diagonal — **forwards or backwards**. Straight lines only.

### What does Hint do?

It helps you find a remaining word (limited uses per puzzle). Use them for the last hard ones.

### What is Large print?

A bigger letter size for easier reading. Toggle it under the puzzle or use the large-print link in the tools row.

### Can I print the puzzle?

Yes — open **[Print](/print)** for a clean worksheet (grid + word list), then tap **Print** or use Cmd/Ctrl+P.

## Content & themes

### Are these official movie / game word packs?

**No.** Default dailies use general vocabulary. We avoid unlicensed franchise packs as product IP.

### Will there be themed packs?

Planned later (for example nature, cities, history with clear rights). Daily play does not depend on them.

### Are the definitions (glosses) complete dictionary entries?

No — short blurbs after you find a word. For study, use a real dictionary.

## Privacy, ads, money

### Do you collect my personal data to play?

Play works **without an account**. Hosting may process standard technical logs (see [Privacy Policy](/privacy)). Theme preferences may stay in your browser only.

### What about cookies / analytics?

You will see a **cookie banner** on first visit. **Analytics stay off** until you accept. Change your mind anytime: footer → **Cookie settings**.

### Are there ads?

Not part of the core experience today. If that changes, we will update the Privacy Policy and this FAQ.

### Is the game free?

**Yes** for daily play. Optional paid extras are not required and are not the current focus.

### Is there a leaderboard?

Not yet. Scores may come later as an optional feature.

## Technical

### The board won’t load / says it could not load the puzzle

- Refresh the page  
- Check that you are online  
- Try again later if the file for “today” is missing (fallback sample may appear)  
- Confirm the date: past days need a generated file under that date  

### Does it work offline?

After assets and today’s JSON are cached by your browser, short offline play may work, but we do not guarantee full offline mode yet.

### Which browsers are supported?

Recent Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge on phone or desktop. Very old browsers may not support the interactions.

### I found a bug or a wrong word path

Note the **date**, device, and what you tried. Then write via [Contact](/contact).
