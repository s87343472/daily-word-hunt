## Get in touch

We read messages about:

- Bugs or broken puzzles  
- Privacy / data requests  
- Content or classroom questions  
- Partnership ideas (non-spam)

### Email

**General & privacy:** [hello@daily-word-hunt.example](mailto:hello@daily-word-hunt.example)

> Replace this address with your real inbox before public launch. Until then, treat it as a template contact point.

### What to include

- The **puzzle date** (YYYY-MM-DD) if relevant  
- Your **browser / device** for play bugs  
- Whether you can share a **screenshot**

### Response time

This project is early. Replies may be slow or batch-processed. For legal notices, put **Privacy** or **Legal** in the subject line.
