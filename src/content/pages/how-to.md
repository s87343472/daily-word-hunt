## Goal

Find every word in the list. Words are hidden in the letter grid in straight lines.

## Controls

### Drag (swipe)

1. Press on the **first letter** of a word.
2. Drag through the letters to the **last letter**.
3. Release. If it matches a remaining word, it lights up.

### Tap then tap (no drag)

1. Tap the **first letter** — it highlights.
2. Tap the **last letter**.
3. If the straight line between them spells a word on the list, it counts.

Tap the same letter again to cancel a selection.

## Allowed directions

Words may run:

- Horizontal or vertical  
- Diagonal  
- **Forwards or backwards**

Only **straight lines** — no bends or zigzags.

## Tips

- Scan for uncommon letters (Q, X, Z) first.
- Check the word list for length — a 7-letter word is easier to rule out.
- Use **Hint** sparingly (limited per puzzle).
- Found a word? Read the short **gloss** under the board for a mini vocab moment.

## Accessibility

- **Large print** — toggle on the puzzle page for bigger letters on screen.
- **Print pack** — open [Print](/print), choose how many sheets (4–12), grid size, and theme, then **Generate pack** → **Print all pages**. One random worksheet per A4 page — built for classrooms, not just “today’s” puzzle.

## Stuck?

- Clear selection and try the other end of the word.
- Words can share letters with already-found words.
- Come back tomorrow — a new daily puzzle unlocks with the calendar.
