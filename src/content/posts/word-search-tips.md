Whether you play on a phone or a laptop, a few habits cut solve time without spoiling the fun.

## 1. Start with rare letters

Q, X, Z, J, and K appear less often. Spot them first, then walk outward.

## 2. Use length as a filter

If the list has one 8-letter word, you’re not hunting every short fragment. Anchor on length.

## 3. Check reverse directions early

Many dailies place words **backwards**. After a miss, try the same line the other way.

## 4. Diagonals last (or first, if you prefer)

Pick a system. Some people clear rows/columns, then diagonals. Consistency beats random scanning.

## 5. Tap-tap when the grid is dense

On small screens, drag can overshoot. Tap the first letter, then the last — same result, often cleaner.

## 6. Save hints for the last two words

Hints are limited. Burn them when the board is noisy and you’re stuck on leftovers.

## 7. Read the gloss

After a find, the short definition is a free micro-lesson — and a pause that prevents rush mistakes.
