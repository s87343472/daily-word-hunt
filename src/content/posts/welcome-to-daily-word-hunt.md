Daily Word Hunt is a free **word search** you can play in the browser.

## Start here

1. Open the [home page](/) — that’s **today’s** puzzle.
2. Find every word on the list by dragging or double-tapping endpoints.
3. Come back tomorrow for a new grid.

You do **not** need a special dated URL. Home always means “today.”

## Word search, not word fill

This is 划词-style play: letters are already on the board; you highlight the path of each word. It is not a rack-building fill game.
